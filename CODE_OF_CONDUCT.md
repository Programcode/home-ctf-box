# Code of conduct

## Our standard

The goal is a place where people learn security without hassling each
other. Be direct, be useful, and assume good faith until evidence says
otherwise.

Behavior that gets you moderated:

- harassment, slurs, or personal attacks;
- posting other people's private information;
- sharing flags, full solutions, or writeups for content under embargo;
- uploading malware, doxxing tools, or anything whose main purpose is
  harming systems or people outside the lab scope;
- using the platform as a staging ground for attacks on real targets.

Security discussion is welcome. Crime is not. Teaching someone how a
buffer overflow works is fine; helping them break into a specific third
party is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers act on reports sent through the GitHub contact for this
repository. Actions range from a private warning to removal of content,
revocation of access, and a ban. Decisions are logged in the moderation
audit trail with reasons. Appeals go to a different maintainer than the
one who issued the action.

This text adapts the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; the enforceable
version is this file.
