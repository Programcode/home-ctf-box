# Regex Doom

The Signal Vault checks entry codes with a regular expression, and your
input becomes the pattern. You will read how the comparison is wired,
craft a pattern that matches any code, and take what the vault holds.

## What you will practice

- Telling the pattern side and the subject side of a regex check apart.
- Writing an overbroad pattern that fullmatches any string.
- Confirming the bypass by capturing the per-session flag.

## Scope

Only the vault at `http://target:8000` is in scope. Nothing outside your
lab instance is a target.
