# Cartographer

Waypoint Maps runs a tidy public website on your lab network. The
operations team keeps its own tooling off the marketing pages, but the
cleanup crew never finished the job. You will map routes no menu links
to, read machine-generated documentation, and collect a secret the
backup pipeline prints for anyone who asks.

## What you will practice

- Treating robots.txt as an inventory of places the site pretends not to have.
- Reading an OpenAPI document to learn request paths, summaries, and
  response shapes before touching the endpoint.
- Deciding when a "blocked in production" claim is worth testing.

## Scope

Only the site at `http://target:8000` is in scope. Nothing outside
your lab instance is a target.
