# Union Vault

GadgetHub sells cables, desk mats, and webcam covers, and its search
box feeds raw text into a SQL query that returns two columns per row.
Somewhere in the same database sits a vault table holding a secret no
storefront page will show you. You will turn the search box into an
extra row of your own design and read the vault out through the product
listing.

## What you will practice

- Reading SQLite errors as evidence of string-concatenated queries.
- Building a UNION SELECT that matches the original column count.
- Confirming the extraction by capturing the per-session flag.

## Scope

Only the shop at `http://target:8000` is in scope. Nothing outside your
lab instance is a target.
