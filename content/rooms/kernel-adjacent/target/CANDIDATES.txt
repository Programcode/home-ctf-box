Kernel-adjacent exploit triage: candidate notes for this lab platform.

Each candidate lists the class of bug and an APPLIES-WHEN line with
machine-readable bounds. Compare those bounds against the FACTS lines
printed by /usr/local/bin/sysinfo. Exactly one candidate survives.

=== Candidate A: CVE-2016-5195 ===
CLASS: copy-on-write write race (Dirty COW class)
APPLIES-WHEN: kernel-min=2.6.22 kernel-max=4.8.99
WHY IT MATTERS: the classic lesson that a version bound alone can
eliminate a famous bug before you touch anything else.
ELIMINATION BASIS: kernel-version bounds only.

=== Candidate B: CVE-2021-4034 ===
CLASS: setuid argument-count confusion in pkexec (PwnKit class)
APPLIES-WHEN: pkexec-present=yes
WHY IT MATTERS: not every local bug depends on the kernel at all;
some depend on a privileged helper simply existing on disk.
ELIMINATION BASIS: presence of a binary, not version ranges.

=== Candidate C: CVE-2021-22555 ===
CLASS: netfilter out-of-bounds write reachable from unprivileged
processes when user namespaces are enabled
APPLIES-WHEN: kernel-min=2.6.19 kernel-max=5.11.99 userns=enabled
WHY IT MATTERS: compound preconditions are common; every listed
condition must hold, not just the version range.
ELIMINATION BASIS: version bounds plus userns availability.

Triage rules for this lab:
1. Facts come from /usr/local/bin/sysinfo only.
2. A candidate applies only if every APPLIES-WHEN condition holds.
3. Record your selected candidate and observed facts honestly; the
   checker cross-validates both against the lab record.
